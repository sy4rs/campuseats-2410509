## Week 5
- Tool(s):ChatGPT

- What I asked for:  
I asked ChatGPT to help me integrate Firebase Authentication into my CampusEats project. I needed help setting up Firebase, creating the AuthProvider and useAuth hook, implementing registration and login pages, handling authentication errors, protecting the Cart and Orders pages, and updating the Header to display the signed-in user's information. I also asked for help upgrading my Recently Viewed Vendors feature to follow the Week 5 requirement for user-specific storage.

- What I kept, changed or rejected, and why:
I kept the suggested Firebase Authentication structure, including AuthProvider, AuthContext, useAuth and ProtectedRoute, because it helped organise authentication across the application. I also used the suggested login, registration and logout implementations. For my personal feature, I kept my existing Recently Viewed Vendors implementation and adapted its local storage key to be specific to each signed-in user. I also used the suggested navigation troubleshooting approach to fix the All Vendors button.

- One thing the AI got wrong and how I fixed it: 
ChatGPT initially treated my Recently Viewed Vendors feature as something that needed to be implemented and tested again. However, my earlier project already contained the basic feature. I asked ChatGPT to check my uploaded ZIP file again, and it identified that the existing implementation was already working. We then focused only on the additional Week 5 requirement of storing recently viewed vendors separately for each user.
